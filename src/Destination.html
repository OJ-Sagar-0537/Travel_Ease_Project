Northern Pakistan Destinations

Gilgit-Baltistan

Hunza

Visit Altit and Baltit Forts, enjoy apricot blossoms in spring. Best time: Mar–Oct.

Skardu

Gateway to K2, Shigar Valley, and Satpara Lake. Best time: May–Sep.

Fairy Meadows

Trek with breathtaking views of Nanga Parbat. Camping only. Best time: Jun–Aug.

Khyber Pakhtunkhwa

Swat

Known as the Switzerland of Pakistan. Mingora and Malam Jabba are top spots. Best time: Mar–Oct.

Kalam

Mountain town with crystal rivers and alpine forests. Best time: May–Sep.

Naran & Kaghan

Famous for Lake Saif-ul-Malook and lush valleys. Best time: May–Sep.
